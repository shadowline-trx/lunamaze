Allergen Label Maker

Privacy

Short version: what you type into the label maker stays on your device. Allergen Label Maker is made by Luna Maze, an independent software studio.

What stays in your browser

Your product names, ingredients and settings are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there when you come back. They are not sent to us. Clear your browser data to remove them.

What we receive if you join the founding list

Your email address, the plan you picked, your business type and how many items you label. We also record which page or ad brought you here (for example the campaign name), so we know what is working. We use this only to email you about the launch of accounts and the New York deadline, and to plan the product. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Your sign-up reaches us either through a form-forwarding service (Web3Forms) or as an email sent from your own email app.

Analytics

We count page views and button clicks (for example "printed a label") with Google Analytics to see whether the tool is useful. Google Analytics sets cookies. It never receives your ingredients, product names or email address. Ad platforms may set their own cookies when you arrive from an ad.
